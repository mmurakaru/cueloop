The client manages a thread's share links as a collection: create a link (public or with a GitHub-login allowlist), update its name and auth in place, copy its line, or revoke one. Owner edits push to every link, live sync follows one link to bound idle cost, and deleting a thread revokes all of its links. The gateway can clear a link's access so a link can be flipped public in place.
