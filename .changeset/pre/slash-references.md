Quick actions in the "/" palette insert a compact reference (a highlighted `/name`) instead of dumping the full prompt into your comment. The reference stays in the comment for you to read, and expands to its full text only when the review is sent to the agent. The palette now lays the name and description out in two columns (the description no longer wraps under the name), ranks matches by a fuzzy score, and tab-completes.
