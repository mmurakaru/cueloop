The share shortcut opens one settings-style dialog: a left nav for share externally / export, a right pane listing the thread's links (each an address with copy and a menu to revoke or open its settings), and a full-body wizard that names a link and toggles a GitHub-login allowlist. Handles read as chips, a private link needs at least one handle before create is allowed, and the public/private choice and manage-access dialogs are gone.
