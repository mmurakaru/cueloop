## One branch per share

A share follows the branch you shared. Revisions and annotations on that branch
remain available to everyone using the SSH command.

## Changes stay synchronized

New annotations appear as collaborators send them. If the connection drops,
cueloop reconnects and catches up.

cueloop combines annotations by identity instead of replacing the full Thread.
One collaborator cannot overwrite another collaborator's notes.

## The owner sends the agent Message

Collaborators can read and annotate the shared Thread. They cannot Approve or
Request changes from your agent. You review the combined feedback and choose
what to send.
